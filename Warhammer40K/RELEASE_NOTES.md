# alpha.7 - static additive UnitDefs

Warhammer definitions and their final names/buildoptions are generated during
release preparation. There is no runtime conversion, rename map, BAR-definition
sandbox, Warhammer post hook or Chobby-specific integration. Hosting processes
ordinary units once, through its own normal loader. Four added commanders retain
startunit=true. Native BAR commanders and build trees remain unchanged.

All models, scripts, textures, weapons/effects and original authoring source are
preserved. The release generator is maintained in the authoring repository.
Normal Hosting restrictions, modifiers and frame quantization now apply directly.
The detailed local comparison records its wreck/terraform/metadata defaults;
it is not a balance expansion or a claim of full faction acceptance.

Current validation is recorded separately. Do not treat local preparation as
public download, WAN, full progression or whole-roster geometry acceptance.
